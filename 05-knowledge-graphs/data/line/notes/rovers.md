# Rovers

## Rovers Missions {#rovers-missions}

Rover R-1, also called the first rover, serves bay 5 and moves a board from the warehouse to pallet 7. StartMission requires the rover to be idle, observed by the Busy flag in the Status folder of R-1. Rover R-2 serves bay 5 too, but it does not serve bay 3. Charge either rover for twenty minutes after four missions.

## Rovers Confirm {#rovers-confirm}

The delivery mission is confirmed by the LastResult flag in the Status folder of R-1. The second rover, also called R-2, can move a cover to pallet 7. It then waits for bay 5 before returning to the charging point. Do not swap the two mission names in the supervisor configuration.

Rover R-1 operating note 1: The transport operator checks missions, charging and the safety scanner before the first cycle. The measured value is written in the shift log and the next operator confirms it at handover.
