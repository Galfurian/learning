# Incidents

## Incident Rover Alias {#incident-rover-alias}

On 4 March, the first rover raised an RFID reader fault on the loop conveyor while moving a board. Rover R-1 was the affected unit, and StopMission on that rover fixed the interruption before the pallet was returned.

## Incident Rover Second {#incident-rover-second}

On 5 March, R-2 reported a screwdriver fault in the Assembly Cell. The second rover was the affected unit, and StopMission on that rover fixed the interruption after the operator checked the fixture.

## Incident Ambiguous {#incident-ambiguous}

On 6 March during the night shift, the rover raised a motor trip alarm on the conveyor. The operator stopped the line, moved the pallet to a safe position, and recorded the event for the morning review. The log names no rover, so the supervisor checked both transport units before the next mission was released.
