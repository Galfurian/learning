# Warehouse

## Warehouse Operations {#warehouse-operations}

The Vertical Warehouse serves bay 5. ExtractTray requires the warehouse to be idle, observed by the Busy flag in the Status folder, and moves a tray to the operator bay. SendBack also moves a tray. Wait ten seconds after the lift stops before opening the bay door. Record the result in the shift log before the next handover.

## Warehouse Reset {#warehouse-reset}

The warehouse alarm reset uses ResetAlarms. Its operation needs the stock-ready condition, observed by the StockReady flag in Inventory, and returns a completion flag. The operator must check that no person is inside the lift before starting a tray cycle. Record the result in the shift log before the next handover.

## Warehouse Detail 2 1 {#warehouse-detail-2-1}

Vertical Warehouse operating note 1: The warehouse operator checks trays, the lift and the operator bay before the first cycle. The measured value is written in the shift log and the next operator confirms it at handover. A normal cycle may pause for inspection when the line is busy, but the operator must not bypass a guard, ignore a warning, or restart equipment while a person is inside the work area.
