# Cnc

## Cnc Route {#cnc-route}

The Engraving Cell serves bay 3 and moves a board. PickBoard requires the CNC cell to be idle, observed by the Busy flag in its Status folder. The arm must approach from the marked side, and the spindle needs a thirty-second warm-up before engraving. Record the result in the shift log before the next handover.

## Cnc Confirm {#cnc-confirm}

The engraving mission is confirmed by the LastResult flag in the Status folder of the Engraving Cell. PlaceBoard moves the board to pallet 7. Inspect the engraving depth every fifty boards and stop the cell if the depth is outside the work instruction. Record the result in the shift log before the next handover.

## Cnc Detail 5 1 {#cnc-detail-5-1}

Engraving Cell operating note 1: The CNC operator checks the spindle, the arm and engraving depth before the first cycle. The measured value is written in the shift log and the next operator confirms it at handover. A normal cycle may pause for inspection when the line is busy, but the operator must not bypass a guard, ignore a warning, or restart equipment while a person is inside the work area.
