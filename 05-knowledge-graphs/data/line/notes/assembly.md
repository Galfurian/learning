# Assembly

## Assembly Route {#assembly-route}

The Assembly Cell serves bay 4 and moves a top cover and a bottom cover onto pallet 7. Assemble requires the cell to be idle, observed by the Busy flag in its Status folder. The two arms must report home before the pallet enters the fixture. Record the result in the shift log before the next handover.

## Assembly Confirm {#assembly-confirm}

The assembly mission is confirmed by the LastResult flag in the Status folder. Screw requires the screwdriver to be ready, observed by the Busy flag while the program runs. Replace the bit after two hundred cycles and record the program number in the shift log. Record the result in the shift log before the next handover.
