# Line

## Line Route {#line-route}

The route is bay 1, then bay 2, bay 3, bay 4, and bay 5. The conveyor hands the pallet to bay 2; bay 2 hands it to bay 3; bay 3 hands it to bay 4; bay 4 hands it to bay 5. A pallet must not skip a bay because the barcode reader checks the route at every stop.

## Line Stops {#line-stops}

An emergency stop in the Assembly Cell stops the conveyor. An emergency stop on the conveyor stops the warehouse. The low air pressure alarm of the conveyor stops the Assembly Cell. The alarm lamp stays on until the cause is removed and the reset method is accepted.

## Line Alternatives {#line-alternatives}

The Loop Conveyor moves pallet 8 during a return cycle. R-1 moves a tray to bay 1, while R-2 moves a board to bay 4. The Board Tester moves a cover during a fixture check. The operator records the return reason before the pallet enters the next bay.

## Line Alternatives Finish {#line-alternatives-finish}

The Engraving Cell moves a top cover during a recovery job. The Assembly Cell moves a board into its fixture, and the Camera Quality Cell moves a cover after inspection. Each movement is recorded against the pallet number. A recovery route may return to bay 4, but it must rejoin the normal route before quality inspection.
