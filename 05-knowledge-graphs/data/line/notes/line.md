# Line

## Line Route {#line-route}

The route is bay:1, then bay:2, bay:3, bay:4, and bay:5. The conveyor next step is bay:2; bay:2 next step is bay:3; bay:3 next step is bay:4; bay:4 next step is bay:5.

## Line Stops {#line-stops}

alarm:assembly/EmergencyStop stops conveyor. alarm:conveyor/EmergencyStop stops warehouse. alarm:conveyor/NoAirPressure stops assembly.
