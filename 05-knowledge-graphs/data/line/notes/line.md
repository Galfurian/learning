# Line

## Line Route {#line-route}

The route is bay 1, then bay 2, bay 3, bay 4, and bay 5. The conveyor next step is bay 2; bay 2 next step is bay 3; bay 3 next step is bay 4; bay 4 next step is bay 5. A pallet must not skip a bay because the barcode reader checks the route at every stop.

## Line Stops {#line-stops}

An emergency stop in the Assembly Cell stops the conveyor. An emergency stop on the conveyor stops the warehouse. The low air pressure alarm of the conveyor stops the Assembly Cell. The alarm lamp stays on until the cause is removed and the reset method is accepted. Record the result in the shift log before the next handover.

## Line Alternatives {#line-alternatives}

The Loop Conveyor also serves bay 3 and bay 4 for maintenance returns. It moves pallet 8 during a return cycle. The Vertical Warehouse serves bay 1 when a replenishment tray is needed. R-1 moves a tray, while R-2 moves a board. The Board Tester moves a cover for a fixture check. The Engraving Cell moves a top cover, the Assembly Cell moves a board, and the Camera Quality Cell moves a cover after inspection.
