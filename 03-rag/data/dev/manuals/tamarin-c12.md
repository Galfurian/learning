# Tamarin C-12 conveyor

## Overview {#c12-overview}

The C-12 is a 12 m belt conveyor with one incline section. It carries parts of up to 20 kg between work cells.

## Belt speed {#c12-speed}

The speed range is 0.1–1.2 m/s. Above 0.8 m/s, parts heavier than 5 kg can slide back on the incline.

## Belt tension {#c12-tension}

Check the tension every month. The belt should deflect 10 mm under a 2 kg weight at mid span. A loose belt slips and squeals.

## Emergency stop {#c12-estop}

Pull cords run along both sides of the conveyor. After an emergency stop, reset the pulled cord, then press START twice within 3 s.

## Error codes {#c12-errors}

E-5102: motor overcurrent. Usually a roller is jammed or the belt is too tight. Lock out power before you clear the jam.
E-5120: the belt slip sensor saw the belt move slower than the drive roller. Check the tension.
E-5012: the photo eye at the exit is blocked for more than 10 s. Remove the part or clean the lens.
E-5201: the drive inverter is too hot. Clean its air filter and check the cabinet fan.

## Maintenance schedule {#c12-maintenance}

Every week, clean the photo eye lenses. Every month, check the belt tension. Every 500 operating hours, grease the roller bearings with lithium grease GR-2.

## Spare parts {#c12-parts}

PN-X7T-991-A: drive roller, 89 mm.
PN-X7T-919-A: idler roller, 60 mm.
PN-X7T-199-A: belt, 12 m, PVC top.
GR-2: lithium grease, 400 g cartridge.

## Troubleshooting {#c12-troubleshooting}

The belt runs off to one side: the tail roller is not square. Adjust the tracking screw by a quarter turn at a time.
The belt squeals at start-up: the tension is too low. See the belt tension section.
Parts slide back on the incline: the belt speed is too high for their weight. Lower the speed below 0.8 m/s.
The conveyor stops without an error code: an emergency stop cord is pulled.
