# Kestrel LX-40 laser cutter

## Overview {#lx40-overview}

The LX-40 is a 40 W CO2 laser cutter with a 600 × 400 mm bed. It cuts and engraves sheet materials. The tube, the chiller and the air assist must all run before a job starts.

## Allowed materials {#lx40-materials}

Cut acrylic, birch plywood and cardboard. Never cut PVC or vinyl: they release chlorine gas that corrodes the optics and harms the operator. Polycarbonate discolours and catches fire easily, so do not cut it either.

## Maximum thickness {#lx40-thickness}

Acrylic up to 8 mm and plywood up to 6 mm in a single pass. Thicker sheets need two passes, with the focus lowered by half the sheet thickness before the second pass.

## Water chiller {#lx40-cooling}

The laser tube is cooled by a closed water loop. Keep the coolant between 15 and 22 °C. Above 25 °C the tube power drops and the tube ages faster. Use distilled water only.

## Air assist {#lx40-air}

Compressed air blows across the cut to remove smoke and stop flames. The nominal pressure is 2.0 bar and the minimum is 1.5 bar. The inline filter cartridge PN-77A keeps oil out of the nozzle.

## Error codes {#lx40-errors}

E-4021: the lid interlock opened during a job. The beam stops at once. Close the lid, check the reed switch on the rear hinge, and press RESUME.
E-4012: air assist pressure is below 1.5 bar. Check the compressor hose and the filter cartridge PN-77A.
E-4120: chiller flow is below 2 litres per minute. Check that the pump runs and that the hoses are not bent.
E-4201: tube current is above 28 mA. Lower the power setting. If the error returns at low power, call service.

## Maintenance schedule {#lx40-maintenance}

Every day, empty the crumb tray. Every week, clean the focus lens with isopropyl alcohol and a lint-free swab. Every 300 operating hours, replace the filter cartridge PN-77A. Every 1000 operating hours, grease the linear rails with grease GR-1.

## Spare parts {#lx40-parts}

PN-X7Q-991-A: focus lens, 38 mm focal length.
PN-X7Q-991-B: focus lens, 50 mm focal length.
PN-X7Q-919-A: first mirror, gold coated.
PN-X7Q-199-A: lens holder tube.
PN-77A: air assist filter cartridge.

## Troubleshooting {#lx40-troubleshooting}

The cut does not go through the sheet: the lens may be dirty, the focus may be wrong, or the sheet may be thicker than the limit. Clean the lens first, then check the focus with the gauge block.
Lines are wavy instead of straight: the X belt is loose. Tighten it until it no longer sags.
The edges are burned and brown: the air assist is too weak. Check the pressure on the gauge.
The beam is weak after a long job: the coolant is probably too warm. Let the chiller bring it back below 22 °C before you continue.
